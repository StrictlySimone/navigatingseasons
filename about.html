ABOUT

Welcome to my warm, nurturing space

I'm Fran, a BACP-accredited integrative and accredited sight loss counsellor offering online counselling across the UK for adults and young people (16+). I specialise in Complex Post Traumatic Stress Disorder (C-PTSD), sight loss, disability support, chronic health conditions, neurodiversity affirming therapy, identity struggles, and life transitions.

Therapy can help if you are coping with a new diagnosis, sight loss, burnout, or the feeling of being unseen or excluded. My approach is trauma-informed, focusing on how past experiences shape your present and providing a safe, supportive space to reflect and grow.

As someone living with sight loss, I bring both professional expertise and lived experience. This means you can expect compassionate, down-to-earth counselling where you feel understood without needing to explain everything.

Working together, we will explore your thoughts and feelings so you can make sense of your experiences, build confidence, and feel more grounded and connected. You do not need to have all the answers before starting counselling. What matters is you can begin your journey at your own pace.

If you are searching for accessible online or telephone therapy in the UK that is inclusive, supportive, culturally affirming and focused on your needs, get in touch today. I am here to listen and help you move forward.
