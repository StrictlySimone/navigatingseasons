HOW I CAN HELP

Support through life's diverse challenges

At Navigating Seasons I'm committed to supporting your emotional well-being through life's diverse challenges. As an experienced counsellor, I specialise in helping you navigate through various seasons of life, allowing you to gain understanding, and strategies to foster personal growth and resilience. Support is available through secure online and telephone sessions.

  • Addiction and Substance Misuse
    Support with overcoming addictive behaviours and developing healthy lifestyles.
  • Anger Management
    Assistance with understanding and managing anger in healthy ways.
  • Anxiety
    Assistance with anxiety disorders.
  • Chronic Health Conditions and Impairments
    Specialised counselling dealing with chronic health conditions or impairments, focusing on emotional coping, adjustment, self-management, and empowerment.
  • Depression
    Support with experiencing and managing depression.
  • Grief and Loss
    Providing guidance and support when dealing with grief and loss.
  • Life Transitions
    Help with navigating life transitions and adjusting to changes effectively.
  • LGBTQIA+ Affirmative Support
    Support for those exploring gender, sexuality, or identity, offered from the perspective of a respectful and affirming ally.
  • Neurodiversity
    Support tailored for neurodivergent clients, including those with autism or ADHD, with a focus on understanding, acceptance, and empowerment.
  • Relationship Issues
    Support in navigating relationship challenges.
  • Self-Esteem and Self-Confidence
    Support in building positive self-image and developing self-confidence.
  • Stress Management & Workplace Burnout
    Specialised tools to help you manage acute stress, recover from professional workplace burnout, set sustainable boundaries, and process the physical impact of long-term exhaustion.
  • Trauma and PTSD
    Assistance with healing from traumatic experiences and managing related symptoms.
