Online and telephone integrative counselling across the UK

For adults and young people aged 16 and over, by video call or by phone.

I’m Fran, a BACP accredited integrative counsellor and Accredited Sight Loss Counsellor. I offer online counselling in the UK by video call on Microsoft Teams or by phone, so you can have your sessions from home or another private space where you won’t be overheard.

My work is trauma-informed. We go at a pace that feels manageable, and you have a say in how we work. I specialise in complex trauma counselling, sight loss, chronic health conditions and neurodiversity-affirming counselling, and I also work with many of the other reasons people come to counselling.

What integrative counselling means

Rather than using one model for everyone, I draw on several approaches and fit them to you. These include Person-Centred and Psychodynamic ideas, CBT, Transactional Analysis, Gestalt and IFS-informed parts work. You don’t need to know anything about them before we start. How I work as an integrative, trauma-informed counsellor explains each in plain language.

Getting started

  • We begin with a free 25-minute initial conversation. You can ask questions and see whether working together feels right, with no obligation.
  • An initial assessment comes before your first counselling session, to explore what brings you to counselling.
  • Sessions last 50 minutes. I usually suggest starting with six sessions, then we review together whether to continue.

The initial assessment is £40 and each 50-minute session is £50. Availability, concessions and the cancellation policy are on the counselling fees and availability page.

Counselling here is not an emergency or crisis service. If you need urgent help, please see crisis support.
