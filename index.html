About Me

Welcome to My Warm, Nurturing SpaceI’m Fran, a BACP-accredited integrative and accredited sight loss counsellor offering online counselling across the UK for adults and young people (16+). I specialise in Complex Post Traumatic Stress Disorder (C-PTSD), sight loss, disability support, chronic health conditions, neurodiversity affirming therapy, identity struggles, and life transitions.Therapy can help if you are coping with a new diagnosis, sight loss, burnout, or the feeling of being unseen or excluded. My approach is trauma-informed, focusing on how past experiences shape your present and providing a safe, supportive space to reflect and grow.As someone living with sight loss, I bring both professional expertise and lived experience. This means you can expect compassionate, down-to-earth counselling where you feel understood without needing to explain everything.Working together, we will explore your thoughts and feelings so you can make sense of your experiences, build confidence, and feel more grounded and connected. You do not need to have all the answers before starting counselling. What matters is you can begin your journey at your own pace.If you are searching for accessible online or telephone therapy in the UK** that is inclusive, supportive, culturally affirming and focused on your needs, get in touch today. I am here to listen and help you move forward.

How I work

I work using an integrative framework tailored specifically to your unique needs. My approach safely blends foundational depth work with active, practical strategies across five core pillars:Person-Centred & Psychodynamic foundations to create a safe, collaborative relationship where we can gently explore past patterns and root causes.
* CBT (Cognitive Behavioural Therapy) to provide you with active, practical tools for managing day-to-day coping mechanisms.
Transactional Analysis (TA) to help you understand relationship dynamics, boundaries, and how you communicate with others.
Gestalt to bring awareness to the present moment and connect with how your body somatically holds stress.This foundational framework integrates directly with an IFS-Informed (Internal Family Systems) Pathway. Together, we use a structured unblending protocol to safely identify body sensations, step back from overwhelming protective responses, and engage in a compassionate dialogue with the parts of you holding deep trauma, neurodivergent exhaustion, or chronic health depletion.

Services and Fees

All sessions are delivered online via Microsoft Teams or phone.Below is a breakdown of the services available:25 Minute Discovery Call - Free.
This call is an opportunity for you to determine whether counselling feels right for you and whether you’d like to work together. We’ll discuss your needs and expectations, and I’ll provide an overview of how the counselling process works. It’s a relaxed space to ask questions and decide if you’d like to move forward with sessions.Initial Assessment Consultation - £40.
The initial assessment helps us explore your needs and goals as we begin working together. We’ll discuss what brings you to counselling and what you hope to gain. The timing of the session is mutually agreed. If you need to cancel for any reason, please review the Cancellation Policy below.Individual Counselling Session - £50 per session.
Regular sessions are held on an agreed day and time each week. During these sessions, we’ll work together to explore your experiences, challenges, and goals. If you need to cancel for any reason, please review the Cancellation Policy below.Limited concessions and supported spaces may be discussed during your initial free 25-minute discovery call based on current diary availability.Availability
I currently offer counselling appointments during the following times:
• Weekday evenings: 6:00 pm – 10:00 pm
• Saturday: 10:00 am – 2:00 pm
Morning appointments (9:00 am – 10:00 am) may be available on request.
All sessions are by appointment only.

Cancellation Policy

If you need to cancel or reschedule your appointment, please notify me at least 24 hours in advance. This allows me to offer the appointment slot to another client who may need it. Cancellations made within 24 hours or missed appointments (DNA) will be charged the full session fee.I understand that emergencies and unforeseen circumstances can arise, and I’ll always consider these on a case-by-case basis. Thank you for your understanding and cooperation. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please feel free to contact me.

Please note: an initial assessment needs to be conducted before the first individual counselling session.

Urgent Help and Crisis Support (24/7)

Navigating Seasons does not provide an emergency or crisis service. If you are experiencing a mental health crisis, feel unsafe, or unable to cope, please use the free, confidential, and 24/7 services listed below.

Immediate Danger – Call 999If life is at risk right now, or you have seriously harmed yourself (e.g., following an overdose or serious injury), please seek immediate medical help.* Call 999: Ask for the Ambulance or Police.
* Go to the nearest Accident & Emergency (A&E) department.
