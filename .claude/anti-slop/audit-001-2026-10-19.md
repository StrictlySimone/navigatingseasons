# antislop audit 001 (19 October 2026)

Mode: after (audit of the site as it is). Nothing has been changed yet.
Pick the numbers you want fixed; anything not picked is left alone.

Kept in `.claude/` so GitHub Pages does not publish it.

## How it was checked

- Read every page, `assets/site.css` and `assets/site.js`.
- Opened all 14 pages in a browser at 360, 390, 820, 1024 and 1440 pixels
  wide, in light and dark mode. Opened and closed the mobile menu with a click
  and with Escape.
- Measured colour contrast with the antislop contrast checker
  (`.claude/skills/antislop-human/contrast-check.py`).

## What already passes

- No made-up numbers, reviews or claims anywhere on the
  site, and the FAQs answer real questions about this practice.
- No page scrolls sideways at any width.
- Keyboard focus is clearly visible on everything. The menu opens and
  closes with Escape. No script errors.
- Almost all text passes the contrast standard in both themes.
- The coloured edge on article cards marks the season, so it carries meaning.
- The wording is clear and personal, with no marketing buzzwords.

## Findings

Priority follows the rule type: HIGH = Hard Gate, MEDIUM = Purpose-Gate,
LOW = Quality Lock.

### HIGH

1. **Dark mode: the "AREAS I SPECIALISE IN" label on the green band is almost
   unreadable** (R-25, R-34). Its contrast is 1.92:1, and normal text needs
   4.5:1. A dark-mode style overrides the white colour meant for that band.
   Fix: make the band's white label win in both themes.
2. **Footer "Site by FlowNarrate" credit is too faint in light mode** (R-25).
   It measures 3.63:1 because it's faded to 75% opacity. Fix: remove the fade.
3. **Contact form boxes are hard to see** (R-25 non-text contrast, C-4). Field
   borders measure 1.32:1 in light mode and 1.47:1 in dark mode, and they
   need 3:1. People with low vision may not be able to find the boxes. Fix: a
   stronger border colour on form fields only.
4. **The footer on every page shows ® (registered trademark), not ©
   (copyright)** (R-36). ® claims "Navigating Seasons" is a registered
   trademark. If it isn't, it should be ©. Needs checking with Fran.
5. **Long dashes (—) in page text** (R-02). They appear in:
   - every page title, the text shown on the browser tab and in Google
     results, such as "About Me — Navigating Seasons";
   - the automatic reply email sent after the contact form;
   - the FormSubmit note under the contact form and in the privacy policy.

   Fix: use "|" in titles, and a comma or full stop in sentences. The privacy
   policy wording is on the "check before changing" list, so that one needs a
   yes from Fran.
6. **Some buttons are smaller than a fingertip** (R-03, minimum 44px).
   - The menu button is 40px.
   - The Light/Dark button is 33px tall.
   - The Contact button in the menu is 37 to 41px.
   - Footer links are about 22px tall.

   Fix: add padding so each one is at least 44px tall. They'll look the same
   size, but be easier to tap.
7. **The top menu spills onto three rows on laptops and tablets** (R-03). Above
   760px wide, the links wrap. "Contact" and the Light/Dark button drop onto
   extra rows, and the header grows to about 185px. The layout only has a
   phone setting and a desktop setting, with nothing in between. Fix: tidy the
   spacing so it fits on one row at wide sizes, and use the menu button on
   tablets and smaller laptops.
8. **There's no written design direction (`DESIGN.md`)** (R-37). It isn't
   visible to visitors, but antislop needs it before any new design work
   ("during" mode). It describes the feel of the site in your own words:
   personality, colours, fonts, mood. You and Fran write it, or answer a few
   questions and I write down your answers. The README already covers fonts
   and colours.

### MEDIUM

9. **Arrows (→) on nine links on the home page** (R-08). When every link has
   one, they stop meaning anything. Fix: keep one or two, or none. This is a
   style choice, so it's your call.
10. **Every card, list item, notice and the form has a drop shadow** (R-12).
    When everything floats, nothing stands out. Fix: keep the shadow on one
    thing per page that should lift (for example the contact form), and make
    the rest flat.
11. **Every section starts with a small label in widely spaced capitals**
    ("GETTING TO KNOW THE PRACTICE", "CREDENTIALS" and so on) (R-06). It's
    the most common AI-template label style, and these are typed in capitals.
    Some screen readers spell typed capitals out letter by letter. Fix: either
    keep the labels with a written reason (they're part of the site's look),
    or cut the ones that repeat the heading under them. Either way, type them
    in normal case and let the style make them capitals, as the Autumn label
    already does. The two badges in the hero are typed in capitals too.
12. **The 🤎 emoji on the Autumn article label** (R-04, emoji as decoration).
    It's hidden from screen readers, so it's harmless. If Fran chose it on
    purpose, it stays. If not, remove it.

### LOW

13. **The home page sections all have the same shape**: label, heading,
    paragraph, cards, divider line (R-05). The green band and the supervision
    box break it up a little. Not urgent, and worth considering only as part
    of design work with a `DESIGN.md`.
14. **The green blob shape and drop shadow behind Fran's photo** (R-31). It
    could be a real motif (organic, seasonal), but nobody has written down why
    it's there. Fix: write one line on why it stays, or simplify it.

## Not included

- Long dashes inside code comments. They aren't visible on the site.
